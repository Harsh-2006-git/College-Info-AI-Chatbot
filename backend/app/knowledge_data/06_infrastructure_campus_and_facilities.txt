================================================================================
MADHAV INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY & SCIENCE (MITS), GWALIOR
06. CAMPUS INFRASTRUCTURE, FACILITIES & HOSTELS
================================================================================

## 1. Campus Overview
- Total Area: 44.6 acres of lush green, eco-conscious campus situated in Gwalior.
- Madhav-Van: In-campus dedicated teak wood forest sanctuary hosting a wide variety of native and migratory birds.
- Digital Infrastructure: High-speed optical fiber backbone across all academic blocks with manageable switches.
- Internet Leased Line: 1,000 Mbps dedicated BSNL leased line providing 24x7 high-speed Wi-Fi and LAN connectivity.
- E-Learning: Institutional MOODLE server launched on 15th August 2017 for learning management, automated online tests, quiz evaluations, and assignment submissions.
- NPTEL Local Chapter: Established on 30th October 2017 providing credit transfer web and video courses across all branches.

## 2. Central Library
- Collection: Over 1,00,000 physical textbooks and reference volumes.
- Digital Consortia: Full-text e-journals from ScienceDirect (Elsevier), ASME, and ASCE under the E-Shodh Sindhu and INFLIBNET consortium.
- Facilities: Automated RFID book issue/return, air-conditioned reading halls, and high-speed digital research lab.

## 3. Hostels & Residential Facilities
- Boys Hostels: 3 well-furnished Boys Hostels with round-the-clock security, hygienic mess, and indoor recreation halls.
- Girls Hostels: 2 dedicated Girls Hostels with gated access control, resident wardens, and high-speed internet.
- Hostel Amenities: Water coolers, desert air coolers, geysers, TV rooms, sports equipment, and Wi-Fi access.

## 4. Student Amenities & Health
- Medical Dispensary: On-campus medical health center with resident doctor and first-aid facilities.
- Banking & Finance: In-campus scheduled commercial bank branch with 24-hour ATM.
- Dining & Cafeterias: Institute Canteen, Amul Ice-Cream Parlour, and Nescafe Coffee Shop.
- Retail: On-campus stationery shop, photocopying center, and general convenience stores.

## 5. Sports & Gymnasium Facilities
- Grounds: Full-size cricket ground, football ground extension, basketball courts, and lawn tennis courts.
- Indoor Facilities: Modern gymnasium, badminton courts, table-tennis facilities, and chess room.
- Competitive Achievements: State and Nodal university champions in Table Tennis, Badminton, Basketball, Kho-Kho, and Football.

## 6. Advanced Research Labs & Specialized Centres
- Drone School: State-of-the-art RPTO (Remote Pilot Training Organization) drone piloting and aviation training facility.
- Entrepreneurship Development Cell (EDC): Established by the Department of Science & Technology (DST), Govt. of India, in 1988; conducts incubation, startup pitching, and entrepreneurship camps.
- Specialized Tech Labs:
  * Robotics and Automation Lab (ASIMOV)
  * Internet of Things (IoT) Sensor Lab
  * Machine Learning & High-Performance Computing (HPC) GPU Lab
  * Augmented & Virtual Reality (AR/VR) Centre
  * Solar Thermal & Hybrid Greenhouse Solar Drying Facility
  * Microgrid and Renewable Energy Integration Facility
