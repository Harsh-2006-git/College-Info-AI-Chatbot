## 2. National Board of Accreditation (NBA) Status
### A. Programs Currently NBA Accredited
The institute holds tier-I/tier-II NBA accreditation for five core undergraduate programs:
1. B.Tech Civil Engineering
2. B.Tech Electrical Engineering
3. B.Tech Mechanical Engineering
4. B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)
5. B.Tech Electronics Engineering

### B. Specialized & Emerging Programs Eligibility Note
Specialized B.Tech programs launched recently (such as IT AI & Robotics, IoT, Mathematics & Computing, AI & Data Science, AI & ML, Computer Science & Design, and CSBS) become eligible for NBA accreditation after the graduation of two full student batches as per NBA accreditation norms.
