## 1. Professional Society Student Chapters (7 Chapters)
MITS hosts active student branches of world-renowned technical societies that organize national hackathons, technical paper contests, and international conferences:
1. IEEE (Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers) Student Branch
2. ACM (Association for Computing Machinery) Student Chapter
3. IET (Institution of Engineering and Technology) Student Chapter
4. CSI (Computer Society of India) Student Chapter
5. IETE (Institution of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineers)
6. ISTE (Indian Society for Technical Education) Students Chapter
7. SAE-INDIA (Society of Automotive Engineers) Collegiate Club

## 2. National Youth & Social Service Wings
- National Cadet Corps (NCC): Active NCC Army and Air wings participating in Republic Day Camps (RDC) and annual training drills.
- National Service Scheme (NSS): Comprehensive social outreach, blood donation drives, environmental tree planting at Madhav-Van, and rural digital literacy camps.
