## 3. Doctoral (Ph.D.) & Post-Doctoral Programs
- MITS is an approved Quality Improvement Programme (QIP) Centre for PG and Ph.D. degrees by AICTE/Ministry of Education.
- Full-time and Part-time Ph.D. programs offered across Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, CSE, IT, Electronics, Mathematics, and Applied Sciences.
- Fellowships: Junior Research Fellowship (JRF) under MPCST funded projects, Institute Fellowships, and Post-Doctoral Fellowships (PDF).

## 4. Key Academic Features & NEP-2020 Implementation
- Multiple Entry & Multiple Exit (MEME): Direct admissions to 2nd and 3rd year UG programs under MEME guidelines.
- Relative Statistical Grading: Implemented across all B.Tech and MCA courses to eliminate exam paper difficulty bias.
- Mandatory Digital Skills: Python, Data Science, and Machine Learning are compulsory foundational courses for all students regardless of engineering branch.
- Minor Degree Specializations: Students from conventional branches can earn Minor Degrees in AI, IoT, Robotics, Data Science, or Cyber Security.
- Novel Engaging Courses (NEC): Compulsory courses in II Semester focused on hands-on practical skills and multidisciplinary development.
- Continuous Evaluation: Weekly LMS quizzes, mid-term evaluations, project exhibitions, and laboratory proficiencies.
