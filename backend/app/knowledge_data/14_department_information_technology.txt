## 2. Department Vision & Mission
### Vision
"To achieve academic excellence in Information Technology and allied fields through quality education, innovative research, and industrial collaboration for societal advancement."

### Mission
1. To provide a rigorous theoretical foundation and hands-on exposure to cutting-edge computing tools.
2. To promote innovation, design thinking, and problem-solving through project-based learning.
3. To collaborate with multinational software enterprises and research organizations for industry readiness.
4. To inculcate professional ethics, teamwork, and lifelong learning attitudes.

## 3. Specialized IT Laboratories
1. Data Science Lab: High-performance computing platform optimized for Python and R programming, big data analytics, and deep learning algorithms.
2. Programming Lab: Modern multi-OS environment supporting C, C++, Java, Full-Stack web development, and open-source software stacks.
3. Computing Lab: Dedicated infrastructure for fast, reliable numerical computing, algorithms design, and simulation.
4. Project Lab: Dedicated space for capstone projects, machine learning prototypes, computer vision, and linear algebra applications.
5. Hardware & Microprocessor Lab: Equipped with digital circuit kits, 8085/8086 microprocessor simulators, microcontrollers, and IoT embedded kits.
6. Research Lab: Advanced laboratory dedicated to doctoral and postgraduate research in natural language processing, network security, and cloud architectures.
