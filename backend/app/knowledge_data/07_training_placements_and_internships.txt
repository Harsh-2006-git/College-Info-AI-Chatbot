## 1. Training & Placement Cell Overview
- Mandate: Bridging academic education with global industry requirements through structured soft-skills training, programming bootcamps, and corporate placement drives.
- Faculty Leadership: Training & Placement Officer (T&P Cell) and Faculty Departmental Placement Coordinators.

## 2. Institutional Internship & Placement Policy
- NEP-2020 Multi-Stage Internship Model:
  1. I Year End: Skill Internship Program (Soft-Skill and Design Thinking Based).
  2. II Year End: Technical Skill Internship Program.
  3. III Year End: 4-Week Research Internship / On-Job Industry Training.
  4. Final Year (VIII Semester): Mandatory Full-Time 6-Month Corporate Internship (December to May). Students work on real-world industrial projects before graduation.
- Pre-Final Year Credit Course: Mandatory credit course on "Professional Skills and Competencies" covering aptitude, coding, group discussions, and technical interviews.
- Interdisciplinary Tech Training: Mandatory core courses in Python, Data Science, and Machine Learning for all engineering disciplines.
