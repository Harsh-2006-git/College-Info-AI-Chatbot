## 2. One-Time & Recurring Mandatory Institutional Charges
- One-time University Development Fee: Rs. 25,000/- (Payable in First Year at admission).
- Caution Money / Security Deposit: Rs. 5,000/- (*100% Refundable after course completion and No-Dues clearance).
- Examination Fee: Rs. 5,000/- per semester (Rs. 10,000/- annually).
- Provisional Degree & Original Degree / Migration Fee: Rs. 10,000/- (Payable in First Year).
- Typical Reporting First Installment Amount at Admission Desk: Rs. 1,24,000/- to Rs. 1,40,000/- (depending on included one-time components).

## 3. Supplementary & Flexible Curriculum Fee Regulations
1. Summer Semester Fee: Rs. 15,000/- per semester for students registering for backlog clearance or CGPA improvement.
2. Multiple Entry & Multiple Exit (MEME) Exit Fee: Rs. 5,000/- payable for provisional certificate/diploma, migration, and transfer certificate for students exiting before the 8th semester after completing credit benchmarks.
3. MOOCs / NPTEL Exam Charges: Applicable registration and exam fees for mandatory NPTEL/SWAYAM credit courses are paid by students directly on national portals.
4. Tuition Dynamic Policy: Tuition fees are determined by the competent authorities and are subject to revision in accordance with government/university regulatory guidelines.
5. Mode of Payment: Demand Draft in favor of "Vice Chancellor MITS DU Self Finance", payable at Gwalior, or online via student ERP/UPI.

## 4. Government & Institutional Scholarships
- Post-Matric Scholarships (SC / ST / OBC): State Government of Madhya Pradesh provides full/partial tuition reimbursement directly to students' Aadhaar-linked bank accounts through the MP TAAS portal.
- Mukhyamantri Medhavi Vidyarthi Yojana (MMVY): Full tuition fee assistance provided by the MP State Government to meritorious students scoring above threshold percentile in 12th Board examinations.
- AICTE Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW) Scheme: Complete tuition fee exemption (only other fees and development fees payable) for top 5% meritorious students of family income < 8 LPA.
- AICTE PG GATE / GPAT Scholarship: Rs. 12,400/- per month stipend awarded to GATE-qualified M.Tech candidates.
- Institutional Research Fellowships: JRF (Junior Research Fellowship) and PDF (Post-Doctoral Fellowship) under MPCST and institute funded research projects.
