## 1. About the Institute
- Official Name: Madhav Institute of Technology & Science (MITS), Gwalior
- Hindi Name: माधव प्रौद्योगिकी एवं विज्ञान संस्थान, ग्वालियर (म.प्र.), भारत
- Status: Institution Deemed to be University (Declared under Distinct Category by Ministry of Education, Government of India, March 2024)
- NAAC Accreditation: Grade A++ (CGPA 3.53) for 5 years (valid from 2023)
- Campus Size: 44.6 acres lush green environment-conscious campus including 'Madhav-Van' (teak wood forest sanctuary)
- Motto: Mission to Innovate Technology for Society

## 2. Historical Milestones & Heritage
- Founded: 1957 by His Highness Sir Jiwaji Rao Scindia, Maharaja of the erstwhile State of Gwalior, under the open-door policy of the Government of India.
- Foundation Stone: Laid by Dr. Rajendra Prasad, the first President of India, on 28th October 1956.
- Building Inauguration: Inaugurated by President Dr. S. Radhakrishnan on 11th December 1964.
- Golden Jubilee: Graced by the President of India, Dr. Pratibha Devi Singh Patil, as Chief Guest on 30th June 2008. A commemorative postal stamp on MITS was released by India Post.
- Deemed University Milestone: Granted Deemed-to-be-University status under Distinct Category by the Ministry of Education, Govt. of India, on UGC advice in March 2024.

## 3. Vision & Mission
### Vision
"To create world class quality Engineers and Technocrats capable of providing leadership in all spheres of life and society."

### Mission
1. To provide quality education in technical and allied disciplines.
2. To organize and arrange innovative courses in Engineering and Technology.
3. To arrange vocational courses in the upcoming fields and innovative subjects to meet global advancement.
4. To promote research in the fields of Technology and Science.

## 4. Message from the Vice Chancellor
- Vice Chancellor: Dr. Rajindra Kumar Pandit
- Key Strategic Focus Areas:
  * Full implementation of National Education Policy (NEP-2020) in letter and spirit across 19 undergraduate disciplines and emerging postgraduate fields.
  * Student-centric academic model: Continuous evaluation, relative statistical grading, summer semesters for backlog clearance/CGPA improvement, credit transfer via MOOCs (NPTEL).
  * Interdisciplinary foundation: Mandatory courses in Python, Data Science, and Machine Learning paired with minor degree options for all engineering branches.
  * Industry Readiness: Credit course on Professional Skills and Competencies in pre-final year capped by a mandatory full-time final semester internship.
  * Hands-on innovation: State-of-the-art laboratories in Robotics, Machine Learning, AR/VR, IoT, and Drone Technology.
  * Research & Doctoral expansion: Expanding funded Ph.D. programs and high-productivity research in national thrust areas.

## 4. Academic Regulations & Guidelines
1. Attendance Mandate: Students must maintain minimum mandatory attendance as per MITS-DU ordinances tracked on the Academic Management System (AMS). Shortage leads to debarment.
2. Compulsory Continuous Assessment: Submitting weekly quizzes, lab assignments, and project deliverables is strictly mandatory.
3. Make-Up Evaluation Policy: Make-up evaluation is strictly permitted only for valid/bonafide documented reasons with advance approval from Class Coordinators and Head of Department. Full syllabus is tested.
4. Relative Grading: Statistical relative grading is applied to all undergraduate courses (ME/M.Tech use standard grading).
5. Summer Semester: An optional fast-track semester conducted at the end of each academic year to allow students to clear backlogs and improve CGPA if below the minimum 5.0 CGPA threshold.
6. Pre-Result Review: Students can formally challenge and apply for pre-result review of major evaluation papers before final grade finalization.

## 2. Program-wise Academic Eligibility Criteria

### A. Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech. - 4-Year Regular / 19 Disciplines)
- Qualifying Exam: 10+2 examination from Madhya Pradesh Board of Secondary Education (MPBSE) or equivalent recognized Central/State Board (CBSE, ICSE, etc.).
- Compulsory Subjects: Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects along with one of Chemistry / Biotechnology / Biology / Technical Vocational subject.
- Minimum Marks Requirement:
  * General / Unreserved (UR) Category: Minimum 45% aggregate marks in qualifying subjects.
  * Reserved Categories (SC, ST, OBC non-creamy layer of MP Domicile): Minimum 40% aggregate marks.
- Basis of Merit & Admission: Joint Entrance Examination (JEE Main) conducted by NTA; vacant seats are subsequently filled through 10+2 qualifying marks merit.

### B. Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch. - 5-Year / 40 Seats)
- Eligibility: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) or 10+3 Diploma with Mathematics.
- Minimum Aggregate: Minimum 50% aggregate marks in 10+2 (45% for MP SC/ST/OBC).
- Mandatory Aptitude Test: Valid score card in the National Aptitude Test in Architecture (NATA) conducted by the Council of Architecture (CoA) or JEE Main Paper 2.

### C. B.Tech. Lateral Entry (Direct 2nd Year / 3rd Semester)
- Eligibility: Passed Diploma in Engineering and Technology with at least 45% marks (40% for MP SC/ST/OBC), OR passed B.Sc. Degree with Mathematics at 10+2 level with at least 45% marks (40% for MP SC/ST/OBC).
- Basis of Merit: Qualifying Diploma / B.Sc. aggregate percentage via MP DTE Lateral Entry Counselling.

### D. B.Tech. under NEP-2020 Multiple Entry & Multiple Exit (MEME)
- Direct Admission to 2nd Year (Semester III) & 3rd Year (Semester V) based on earned academic credits from ABC (Academic Bank of Credits) and qualifying certificate/diploma as per UGC NEP-2020 guidelines.

### E. B.Tech. for Working Professionals (Flexible Shift)
- Eligibility: Polytechnic Diploma in relevant branch with min 45% marks (40% for SC/ST/OBC) + Minimum 1 year full-time regular professional work experience + Workplace located within 75 km radius of the institution.

### F. Master of Technology (M.Tech. / M.E. - 11 Specializations)
- Eligibility: B.E. / B.Tech. or equivalent degree in relevant discipline from AICTE recognized institution with minimum 50% aggregate marks (45% for MP SC/ST/OBC).
- Basis of Merit: Preference given to valid GATE qualified candidates; remaining seats allocated based on B.Tech. qualifying percentage.

### G. Master of Computer Applications (M.C.A. - 2-Year)
- Eligibility: Passed BCA / Bachelor Degree in Computer Science Engineering or passed B.Sc./B.Com./B.A. with Mathematics at 10+2 level or at Graduation level, with at least 50% marks (45% for MP SC/ST/OBC).
- Basis of Merit: MP DTE Online Counselling merit list.

### H. Master of Business Administration (MBA - 2-Year)
- Eligibility: Recognized Bachelor's Degree of minimum 3 years duration in any discipline with at least 50% aggregate marks (45% for MP SC/ST/OBC).
- Basis of Merit: Valid CMAT (Common Management Admission Test) score conducted by NTA followed by qualifying graduation marks.

### I. Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.)
- Eligibility: Master's degree in relevant discipline with minimum 55% marks (50% for SC/ST/OBC/PwD).
- Selection Process: Institute Ph.D. Pre-Qualifying Entrance Examination (PPQE) followed by Presentation and Interview. GATE/NET/JRF qualified candidates are exempted from written test.
