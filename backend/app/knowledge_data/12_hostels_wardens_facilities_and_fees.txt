## 4. Hostel Rules & Standard Operating Procedures (SoPs)
1. Duration Benchmark: One semester is officially considered as five (05) months duration.
2. Non-Refundable Nature: Hostel fee and development fee are non-refundable semester charges, not monthly rentals.
3. Advance Mess Adjustment: Mess charges are collected as an advance; monthly bills are reconciled according to actual contractor charges.
4. Security Deposit Refund: Caution money (Rs. 10,000) is refunded upon degree completion/hostel exit after submitting a No-Dues certificate, subject to any property damage or disciplinary deductions.
5. In-Out Timings: Strict biometric attendance and hostel curfew timings are maintained at all hostel gates for student safety.
