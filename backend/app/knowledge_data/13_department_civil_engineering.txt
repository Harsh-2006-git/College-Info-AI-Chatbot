- Established: 1957 (One of the pioneer founding departments of MITS Gwalior).
- Head of Department: Dr. Sanjay Tiwari (Professor & Head) | Phone: 0751-2409351 | Email: stiwari.fce@mitsgwalior.in
- Degree Programs Offered:
  * B.Tech. in Civil Engineering (4-Year UG Program) — Annual Intake: 60 seats.
  * M.E. in Construction Technology & Management (CTM - 2-Year PG) — Intake: 18 seats.
  * M.Tech. in Structural Engineering (2-Year PG) — Intake: 18 seats.
  * Ph.D. in Civil Engineering (Full-time & Part-time under QIP / Institute / RGPV).
- Accreditation: B.Tech. in Civil Engineering is accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA) for 6 years w.e.f. July 2021.
- QIP Centre: Recognized Quality Improvement Programme (QIP) center for PG and Ph.D. programs under mentorship of premier IITs.
- Active MoUs: Central Road Research Institute (CRRI New Delhi), Central Building Research Institute (CBRI Roorkee), National Highways Authority of India (NHAI), and Gwalior Smart City Development Corporation Limited.
- Professional Societies: Active institutional chapters of Indian Concrete Institute (ICI) and Indian Water Works Association (IWWA).

## 2. Department Vision & Mission
### Vision
"To produce civil engineers with high technical competencies, research attitude, and ethical values to serve the society and environment."

### Mission
1. To provide high-quality education through updated curriculum aligned with NEP-2020.
2. To encourage research, consultancy, and innovation addressing regional and national infrastructure challenges.
3. To foster strong academia-industry partnerships through field visits, expert lectures, and practical internships.
4. To instill ethical practices, environmental consciousness, and leadership qualities.

## 3. Specialized Departmental Laboratories
The department houses modern testing equipment procured under TEQIP II, TEQIP III, AICTE, and State Research grants:
1. Concrete Technology & Material Testing Lab (Compression testing, rebound hammer, ultrasonic pulse velocity)
2. Geotechnical & Soil Mechanics Lab (Triaxial shear, direct shear, consolidation, CBR apparatus)
3. Environmental Engineering & Pollution Testing Lab (Spectrophotometer, BOD/COD incubators, air quality monitors)
4. Transportation & Highway Engineering Lab (Ductility, penetration, flash/fire point, Los Angeles abrasion)
5. Surveying & Geomatics Lab (Total Stations, Digital Theodolites, Auto Levels, GPS receivers)
6. Structural Engineering & Dynamics Lab (Shake table simulation, loading frames)
7. Hydraulics & Fluid Mechanics Lab (Flow measurement flumes, pumps, turbines)
8. Computer-Aided Civil Design (CAD) Lab (STAAD.Pro, AutoCAD, ETABS, GIS)
