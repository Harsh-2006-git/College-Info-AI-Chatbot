ABOUT SDC
================================================================================
Who We Are:
A community of passionate developers, innovators, and tech enthusiasts building the future together at MITS Gwalior.

Our Mission:
To empower students with practical software development skills, foster innovation through collaborative projects, and bridge the gap between academic learning and industry requirements. We believe in learning by doing and growing together as a community.

Our Vision:
To become the leading platform for student developers to learn, collaborate, and innovate. We envision a future where every student has the skills and confidence to build amazing software that solves real-world problems and makes a positive impact.

What We Do (Activities):
- Workshops: Regular coding workshops and tech talks on the latest technologies.
- Hackathons: Organize and participate in competitive coding events and campus hackathons.
- Mentorship: Peer-to-peer learning and guidance from experienced members and alumni.
- Open Source: Contribute to open source projects and build strong developer portfolios.
- Campus Portals: Build and maintain core digital infrastructure, automation tools, and portals for MITS Gwalior.

SDC By The Numbers:
- 50+ Members
- 10+ Live Deployed Production Projects
- 5+ Events Organized
- 20+ Tech Stacks
- 1000+ Active Student & Faculty Users
- 99.9% Infrastructure Uptime

Tech Stack & Technologies Mastered:
- Modern Stack: React, Next.js, Node.js, Django, TypeScript
- Production Ready: Docker, Kubernetes, Git, CI/CD
- Full Stack & Databases: MongoDB, PostgreSQL, Tailwind CSS, Three.js
