Student Life — Student Activities
National Cadet Corps (NCC)
Madhav Institute of Technology & Science (MITS), Gwalior

Overview:
Madhav Institute of Technology & Science has an independent technical unit of NCC named "3 MP TEC (I)". The NCC organization is committed to grooming cadets as responsible, disciplined, and motivated citizens, ready to provide leadership in all fields.

Categories of NCC Activities:
1. Institutional Training
2. Personality Development
3. Community Development Programme cum Social Service Activities
4. Youth Exchange Programs
5. Adventure Training and Sports
