OFFICE ORDER:
In compliance with the statutory provisions of:
1. The Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 (कार्यस्थल पर महिलाओं का लैंगिक उत्पीड़न निवारण, प्रतिषेध एवं प्रतितोष अधिनियम 2013).

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES & FUNCTIONS OF ICC:
1. To implement and enforce the provisions of the POSH Act 2013 and UGC Regulations 2015.
2. To receive, investigate, and address complaints of sexual harassment and gender-based discrimination submitted by women students, faculty, or staff.
3. To conduct fair, time-bound, and confidential inquiries into reported grievances.
4. To organize gender sensitization workshops, orientation programs, and legal awareness campaigns across campus.
5. To recommend preventive measures, counseling services, and policy updates to maintain a secure and respectful campus environment.

TENURE:
The tenure of the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) members shall be 3 years (up to 2026-2027), while student representatives have a tenure of 1 year.
