Facilities Available at Medical Dispensary:
1. OPD Consultation:
   Free of charge for all staff members, their dependants, all students and their dependants.
2. Dispensing of Medicines:
   Emergency medicines, I.V. fluids, and simple analgesics provided free of cost.
3. Injections, Dressings, Minor Surgical Procedures:
   Performed free of charge for all residents and inmates of the MITS campus as and when necessary.
4. Day Care Facilities:
   Admission of selected patients for administering I.V. fluids under observation.
5. Blood Sugar Testing:
   Done via glucometer free of cost for students and staff.
6. ECG:
   Conducted using BPL ECG machine free of cost as and when required.
7. Vaccinations:
   Provided at nominal cost on a no-profit-no-loss basis for all students, staff, and dependants.
8. Health Awareness Lectures:
   Organized regarding lifestyle and common diseases such as Diabetes, Hypertension, etc.
9. Nebulizer:
   Ultrasonic Nebulizer treatment provided free of cost for all students and staff.
