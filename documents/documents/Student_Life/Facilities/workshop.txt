Student Life — Facilities
Central Workshop
Madhav Institute of Technology & Science (MITS), Gwalior

Overview:
The workshop is the heart of the engineering curriculum in general, and practically for the Mechanical Engineering discipline. Here, students are exposed to various manufacturing processes with critical study of process parameters. Central Workshop, a core unit of Madhav Institute of Technology & Science, Gwalior, was established in 1957. The workshop possesses spacious working areas, sophisticated machinery, and tools for performing diverse practical experiments.

Academic Role:
Basic training in the workshop satisfies degree requirements for all engineering branches during their first or second semester. In addition, practical classes for B.E./B.Tech (Mechanical Engineering) and M.Tech (Production Engineering) are conducted with the guidance of experienced faculty and technical staff in various specialized shops.

Equipment & Upgrades:
- Advanced Machines: Electro Discharge Machining (EDM), Electro Chemical Machining (ECM), and Plasma Cutting machines are installed alongside conventional machine tools.
- Modern Upgradation: Installation of NC and CNC machines with ongoing renovation to meet contemporary industry 4.0 standards.
- Industrial Interaction: Supports industrial product development and fosters strong industry-institute interaction.

Institute Maintenance Services:
The Central Workshop undertakes regular maintenance activities across the campus:
- Electrical, welding, and carpentry repairs for the institute, student hostels, staff quarters, faculty cabins, and departmental laboratories.
- Manages electric and water supply distribution, including diesel generator backup power systems and inventory control for campus infrastructure maintenance.

Specialized Shops in Central Workshop:

2. Foundry Shop
3. Fitting Shop
4. Welding Shop
5. Carpentry Shop
