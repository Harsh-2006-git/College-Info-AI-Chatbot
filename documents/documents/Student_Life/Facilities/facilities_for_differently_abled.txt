Student Life — Facilities for Differently-abled (Divyangjan)
Criterion 7.1.7: Disabled-Friendly, Barrier-Free Environment
Madhav Institute of Technology & Science (MITS), Gwalior
(Deemed to be University)

OVERVIEW:
The layout and infrastructure of MITS Gwalior are designed to ensure an inclusive, barrier-free, and disabled-friendly environment, enabling differently-abled individuals (Divyangjan) to move freely and independently both within and outside campus buildings.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUMMARY OF FACILITIES & INFRASTRUCTURE:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Built environment with ramps and lifts for easy access to classrooms.
2. Divyangjan-friendly accessible washrooms.
3. Signage including tactile paths, lights, display boards, and signposts.
4. Assistive technology and facilities for Divyangjan: accessible website, screen-reading software, mechanized equipment.
5. Provision for enquiry and information: human assistance, reader, scribe, soft copies of reading material, screen reading.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. BUILT ENVIRONMENT WITH RAMPS & LIFTS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Campus buildings are equipped with ramps and passenger lifts to ensure unobstructed movement for students, staff, and visitors with locomotor disabilities.
- Locations with Ramps:
  * Main Entry of the Institute
  * Architecture Department
  * Mechanical Engineering Department
  * Seminar Halls & Conference Auditoriums
- Locations with Lifts:
  * Student Hostels
  * Architecture Department & Multi-Storey Academic Blocks

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DIVYANGJAN-FRIENDLY WASHROOMS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Specially designed, spacious, barrier-free restrooms with grab rails, anti-skid flooring, low-height washbasins, and emergency call support are available across key academic blocks and hostel premises.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. SIGNAGE (TACTILE PATHS, LIGHTS, DISPLAY BOARDS & SIGNPOSTS):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Prominent signage, tactile pathways, illuminated indicators, and directional display boards guide students to vital facilities:
  * Central Library
  * Counselor Office
  * Accounts & Administrative Office
  * Designated Parking Areas

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. ASSISTIVE TECHNOLOGY & DIGITAL ACCESSIBILITY:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Accessible Website (Domain: https://mitsgwalior.in/):
  * Complies with World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.0 Level AA.
  * Compatible with standard screen reading software for visually impaired users.
- Text Size Adjustment:
  * Users can adjust font scale using top bar controls:
    - A- : Decrease text size
    - A  : Standard default text size
    - A+ : Increase text size
- High Contrast Display Scheme:
  * Contrast toggle icons allow switching between:
    - High Contrast View: Black background with high-contrast text for visual clarity.
    - Standard Contrast View: Standard campus theme.
- Specialized Assistive Software & Hardware:
  * Indian Sign Language (ISL) software in Central Library.
  * Screen reading software integrated with Braille keyboards for visually challenged students.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. PROVISION FOR ENQUIRY, INFORMATION & PERSONAL ASSISTANCE:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Wheelchair Provision: Maintained at entrance gates and health centers for immediate mobility assistance.
- Human Assistance & Readers: Dedicated volunteers and staff available for guiding and reading study material.
- Scribe Facility: Provision of certified scribes during internal tests and end-semester university examinations as per UGC/AICTE examination norms.
- Digital Learning Material: Soft copies of books, lecture notes, syllabus, and audio materials for screen reading.
