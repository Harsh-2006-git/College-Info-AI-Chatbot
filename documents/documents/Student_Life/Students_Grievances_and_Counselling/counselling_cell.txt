About the Counselling Cell:
Counselling is a structured method to facilitate mental and emotional well-being and the overall personal and academic growth of students through guidance and professional psychological support for a healthy mind and body.

To address and help in resolving the emotional and psychological issues of students, the Counselling Cell encourages and motivates students to be self-aware, address their challenges, and guides them in resolving personal, emotional, social, family, peer, and academic difficulties.

Services & Objectives:
- Individual and Group Counselling sessions
- Guidance on achieving academic and career goals
- Social and personality development
- Enhancing active listening skills, empathy, and interpersonal communication
- Building healthy interpersonal relationships and emotional resilience
- Fostering the holistic growth and well-being of every student
