OFFICE ORDER:
In compliance with the directives of the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India and AICTE Regulations notified vide No. F. DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Ensure zero tolerance towards any act of ragging anywhere on campus, in hostels, and campus surroundings.
- Conduct surprise inspections of hostels, canteen, bus stops, recreational areas, and student lounges.
- Provide counseling to freshers and ensure a welcoming, supportive atmosphere.
- Immediately examine and report any incident of indiscipline to the Proctor and the Vice Chancellor.
