MANDATE & OBJECTIVES:
The Research Advisory Cell will:
1. Formulate the overarching research roadmap and innovation strategy of MITS-Deemed to be University.
2. Review and monitor the progress of research projects, patents, and sponsored R&D initiatives.
3. Foster interdisciplinary collaborations, international publications, and extramural research grants from agencies such as DST, AICTE, SERB, DRDO, and ISRO.
4. Establish quality benchmarks for Ph.D. research and promote research ethics across all departments.
