OVERVIEW:
Madhav Institute of Technology & Science (MITS), Deemed University, Gwalior, has successfully organized seven editions of the flagship International Conference on Sustainable and Innovative Solutions for Current Challenges in Engineering & Technology (ICSISCET - 2019 to 2025), four editions of the International Student Conference on Multidisciplinary and Current Technical Research (ISCMCTR - 2023 to 2026), and the IEEE International Conference on Electrical, Communication, and Computing Technologies (iCONECCT-2025). These international forums bring together global researchers, academicians, and industry experts to disseminate cutting-edge research in computational sciences, sustainable technology, and engineering innovation.
