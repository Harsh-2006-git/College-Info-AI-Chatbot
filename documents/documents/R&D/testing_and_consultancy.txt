R&D — Testing & Consultancy
Madhav Institute of Technology & Science (MITS), Gwalior
(Deemed to be University)

OVERVIEW:
MITS Gwalior is a recognized hub for industrial testing, calibration, design vetting, and technical consultancy services across Madhya Pradesh and Central India. The institute leverages its state-of-the-art laboratories and domain-expert faculty to deliver specialized consultancy for government bodies, public sector undertakings, and private enterprises.

MAJOR CONSULTANCY DOMAINS & SERVICES:
1. Civil & Structural Engineering:
   - Soil investigation & geotechnical testing.
   - Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) of concrete structures, bridges, and buildings.
   - Material testing: cement, steel, coarse/fine aggregates, bitumen, and bricks.
   - Structural design verification and vetting for public infrastructure projects.
   - Third-party quality inspection and monitoring for government municipal corporations, PWD, NHAI, and smart city projects.

2. Electrical & Electronics Engineering:
   - Energy auditing and electrical power quality analysis.
   - High-voltage testing and calibration of electrical instruments.
   - Embedded systems and IoT prototyping for industrial automation.

3. Mechanical Engineering:
   - Vibration analysis, acoustic measurement, and dynamic balancing.
   - Thermal efficiency analysis of boilers and heat exchangers.
   - Material characterization, tensile testing, and hardness evaluation.

4. Environmental & Chemical Engineering:
   - Water and wastewater quality analysis (BOD, COD, heavy metals).
   - Air pollution monitoring and environmental impact assessments.

5. Computer Science & Information Technology:
   - Enterprise software design, security auditing, and cloud infrastructure setup.
   - AI-driven analytics, automation, and computer vision solutions.

KEY CLIENTELE & COLLABORATORS:
- Smart City Gwalior
- National Highways Authority of India (NHAI)
- MP Public Works Department (PWD) & Municipal Corporations
- MP Urja Vikas Nigam & MP Discoms
- Defence Research and Development Establishment (DRDE)
- Private construction and manufacturing firms across the region.
