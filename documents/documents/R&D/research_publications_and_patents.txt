R&D — Research Publications & Patents
Madhav Institute of Technology & Science (MITS), Gwalior
(Deemed to be University)

OVERVIEW:
Research and innovation form the cornerstone of MITS Gwalior's academic vision. Faculty members, research scholars, and students actively engage in high-impact research across diverse multidisciplinary domains resulting in peer-reviewed journal publications in SCI, SCIE, and Scopus indexed journals, as well as national and international patents.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS:
- High-Impact Research Publications: Over hundreds of research articles published in top-tier international journals indexed in Web of Science, SCI/SCIE, Scopus, IEEE Xplore, Springer, Elsevier, Wiley, and Taylor & Francis.
- Patents (Granted & Published): Faculty and student inventors have been granted and published numerous utility and design patents in fields such as:
  * Advanced materials & blended composites
  * IoT & embedded smart sensors
  * Artificial Intelligence & machine vision systems
  * Renewable energy & solar forecasting
  * Environmental and wastewater treatment technologies
- External Research Funding & Sponsored Projects:
  * Research grants received from central agencies including AICTE (RPS, MODROBS), DST, SERB, CSIR, and MP Council of Science and Technology (MPCST).
- Faculty Publication Incentives:
  * Institute awards meritocracy recognition and financial incentives to faculty for publishing in high-impact-factor journals (Q1/Q2 SCIE/Scopus).
- Research Portal:
  * Faculty research profiles, citation indexes (Google Scholar, Scopus, Vidwan, ORCID) are monitored through the institute's research database.
