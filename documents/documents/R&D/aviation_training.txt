INSTITUTIONAL DESCRIPTION:
================================================================================
Madhav Institute of Technology & Science (MITS), Gwalior, India was established in 1957 by His Highness Sir Jiwaji Rao Scindia, Maharaja of the erstwhile state of Gwalior under the open door policy of the Government of India.
- Foundation Stone: Laid by the then President of India, Dr. Rajendra Prasad, on 20th October, 1956.
- Inauguration: Inaugurated by President Dr. S. Radhakrishnan on 11th December, 1964.
- Golden Jubilee: Graced by the President of India, Dr. Pratibha Devisingh Patil, as Chief Guest on 30th June, 2008, where a commemorative Indian postal stamp on MITS was officially released.

MISSION & VISION:
================================================================================
Mission:
- To provide quality education in technical and allied disciplines.
- To organize and arrange innovative courses in Engineering and Technology.
- To arrange vocational courses in upcoming fields and innovative subjects to meet global advancement.
- To promote research in the fields of Technology and Science.

Vision:
To create world-class quality Engineers and Technocrats capable of providing leadership in all spheres of life and society.
