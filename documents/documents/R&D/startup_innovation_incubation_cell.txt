R&D — Start-up, Innovation & Incubation Cell
Madhav Institute of Technology & Science (MITS), Gwalior
(Deemed to be University)

OVERVIEW:
The Start-up / Innovation / Incubation Cell at MITS Gwalior is committed to promoting an entrepreneurial mindset and facilitating product development from conceptualization to commercialization.

MISSION:
To create a self-sustainable platform for all the stakeholders in and around MITS willing to unleash their potential as idea progenitor and executor.

VISION:
Our vision is to create a hub of opportunities and resources for young ideators in central India.

OBJECTIVES:
- Foster a spirit of entrepreneurship and innovation among students and faculty members.
- Nurturing start-ups at an early-stage and helping them overcome technical, operational, and financial obstacles.
- Promote the culture of Product Development, Innovation, Entrepreneurship, Business Development, and Start-ups.
- Offer value-added and incremental services viz. financial, technical, legal, IPR (Intellectual Property Rights), and mentoring to students, faculty, and staff.
- Offer a variety of resources and networking opportunities to support MITS Gwalior young entrepreneurs on the path of commercialization.
- Move innovative ideas from the institute laboratory to the commercial market.

START-UP CELL STUDENT SOCIETY:
The Start-up Cell Student Society has worked remarkably and proficiently in inspiring, developing, and supporting entrepreneurial ideas and founders inside and around MITS. As a result, a vibrant innovation ecosystem has emerged at MITS Gwalior.
Students participating in the Start-up Cell gain:
- Direct hands-on experience in running entrepreneurial ventures, organizing ideathons, hackathons, and pitch fests.
- Industry mentorship, angel investor networking, and incubator linkages.
- Comprehensive knowledge of leadership, business modeling, IPR protection, marketing, product design, content development, and event management.
- Certificates of appreciation, project experience, and formal letters of recommendation.
