R&D — Research Policies & Guidelines
Madhav Institute of Technology & Science (MITS), Gwalior
(Deemed to be University)

OVERVIEW:
To maintain high academic standards, promote ethical research, and encourage innovation, MITS-Deemed to be University has established institutional research policies governing faculty research, seed grants, intellectual property, and doctoral studies.

KEY POLICIES & FRAMEWORKS:
1. Research Promotion & Seed Grant Policy:
   - Financial seed grants provided to newly joined and existing faculty members to initiate experimental work and preliminary investigation for extramural project proposals.

2. Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) & Patent Policy:
   - Comprehensive framework for the disclosure, evaluation, patent filing, and commercial licensing of institutional innovations.
   - 100% financial reimbursement for official patent filing and examination fees for institute-affiliated inventors.

3. Research Incentive Scheme:
   - Annual cash rewards and meritocracy citations awarded to faculty members on MITS Day for publishing in SCI / SCIE / Scopus indexed Q1/Q2 journals.

4. Conference & Travel Grant Policy:
   - Financial support provided to faculty members and research scholars to present accepted research papers at reputed national and international conferences.

5. Code of Ethics in Academic Research & Plagiarism Guidelines:
   - Zero tolerance policy for academic misconduct, data falsification, and plagiarism.
   - Mandatory similarity check via Turnitin prior to all thesis, project, and manuscript submissions in accordance with UGC Regulations.

6. Consultancy & Testing Guidelines:
   - Transparent rules for faculty involvement, revenue sharing, testing fee structures, and utilization of institute laboratory equipment for industrial consultancy projects.
