Madhav Institute of Technology & Science (MITS), Gwalior
(Deemed to be University)

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (ADMISSIONS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q1: What is the admission process for B.Tech at MITS Gwalior?
A: B.Tech admissions at MITS are conducted primarily through JEE Main scores and Madhya Pradesh state online counselling conducted by Directorate of Technical Education (DTE MP). Key steps include:
   1. Appear in the JEE Main examination.
   2. Register for MP DTE online counselling at https://dte.mponline.gov.in.
   3. Fill branch and institute choices (select MITS Gwalior and preferred branches).
   4. Attend document verification and receive seat allotment.
   5. Report to MITS, submit required documents, and pay the admission fee.

Q2: What is the admission process for B.Arch?
A: Admissions to Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch) are based on NATA scores and/or JEE Main Paper 2 scores through DTE MP online counselling.

Q3: What are the admission criteria for PG programs (M.Tech, MCA, MBA)?
A:
- M.Tech / M.E.: Based on GATE scores and qualifying degree marks through DTE MP counselling.
- MCA: Based on MP Pre-MCA test or qualifying graduation merit through DTE MP.
- MBA: Based on CMAT / qualifying graduation scores through DTE MP counselling.

Q4: What documents are required at the time of admission?
A: Candidates must bring original and photocopies of:
   - 10th and 12th Marksheets & Passing Certificates
   - Entrance Exam Score Card (JEE Main / GATE / NATA / CMAT / Pre-MCA)
   - MP DTE Seat Allotment Letter
   - Transfer Certificate (TC) & Migration Certificate
   - Character Certificate
   - Caste Certificate, Income Certificate & Domicile Certificate (if applicable)
   - Aadhaar Card & Passport size photographs
   - Medical fitness certificate
   Detailed list: https://web.mitsgwalior.in/images/2026/july/Admission%20documents_compressed.pdf

Q5: What scholarships are available for students at MITS?
A:
- SC/ST/OBC Post-Matric Scholarships via the MPTAASC portal.
- Mukhyamantri Medhavi Vidyarthi Yojana (MMVY) by the Government of MP (refer to https://scholarship.mitsgwalior.in).
- Gaon Ki Beti Yojana for eligible female students from rural areas.
- Pragati & Saksham Scholarships by AICTE.
- Merit scholarships and tuition fee waivers under the TFW (Tuition Fee Waiver) Scheme.

Q6: Is hostel accommodation available for first-year students?
A: Yes, hostel facilities are available for both boys and girls with 24x7 security, mess, Wi-Fi, recreation hall, and sports facilities. Hostel registration can be done via Google Form: https://forms.gle/b7Wm3Zjv8i2KDUHv7.
