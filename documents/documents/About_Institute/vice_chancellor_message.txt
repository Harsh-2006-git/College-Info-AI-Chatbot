Vice Chancellor's Message

Vice Chancellor
Dr. Rajindra Kumar Pandit

From the Vice Chancellor’s Desk………
Dear Students, Parents, Alumni, and Corporate Partners,

It brings me immense joy to share that Madhav Institute of Technology and Science (MITS), Gwalior, has successfully completed two years as a Deemed-to-be-University. In March 2024, the Ministry of Education, Government of India, on the advice of the UGC, granted us this prestigious status. Since our inception in 1957, MITS has proudly served the nation. Today, this milestone allows us to leverage academic autonomy to scale new heights of excellence for all our stakeholders.

To Our Students and Parents: A Holistic Ecosystem
We are committed to nurturing well-rounded individuals through a dynamic curriculum that implements the National Education Policy (NEP-2020) in letter and spirit. We offer undergraduate programs across 19 disciplines, alongside cutting-edge B.Tech and newly launched Post-Graduate programs in emerging areas like Artificial Intelligence, Embedded Systems & VLSI, and the Internet of Things (IoT).
Our unique continuous evaluation model, relative grading, summer semesters, and credit transfers from MOOCs (NPTEL) encourage self-learning. Furthermore, our mandatory courses in Python, Data Science, and Machine Learning—paired with minor degree options—ensure that every student, regardless of their core branch, can thrive in an interdisciplinary world. Beyond academics, our vibrant, green campus and active student clubs ensure personal growth, leadership development, and options to develop excellent communication skills.

To Our Corporate Partners: Industry-Ready Professionals
MITS is dedicated to producing engineers who can solve real-world challenges from day one. Our student-centric roadmap includes a dedicated credit course on Professional Skills and Competencies in the pre-final year. This is capped by a mandatory, full-time final-semester internship that provides students with practical corporate exposure before graduation.
Our students learn by doing. Equipped with state-of-the-art labs in Robotics, ML, AR/VR, and IoT, they actively transition from learners to innovators. This is proven by their consistent victories at premier global hackathons and their ability to build in-house digital portals that streamline our university operations, saving significant revenue and man-hours.

To Our Global Alumni Network: A Legacy of Excellence
Our alumni are our greatest pride. You occupy highly placed, influential positions across global corporates, public sectors, railways, armed forces, administrative services, and academia. As MITS transitions into this distinct university era, your legacy remains our anchor. We are expanding our Ph.D. programs in thrust areas and funding high-productivity research to ensure your alma mater remains a globally recognized hub of innovation.

Looking Ahead
The Deemed-to-be-University status has allowed us to elevate our quality initiatives, fulfilling the national mandate of providing a holistic technical education that strengthens India's global footprint. On behalf of the management, faculty, and staff, I welcome our current and prospective students, and I wish you all the very best in your present and future endeavours.
