Duties and Responsibilities

Dean, Faculty of Engineering & Technology:
o The Dean Faculty of Engineering & Technology will be the academic head of the MITS-DU.
o The Dean FoET will have the right to approve & forward the papers/documents of faculty & staff related to academics /HR development etc. (excluding service-related documents).
o The Dean FoET will prepare the institutional development plan (lDP) for the future.
o The Dean FoET will overall monitor the accreditation of institution/programmes by different agencies and coordinate with the various committees.
o The Dean FoET is authorized to conduct / chair meetings of MITS-DU on behalf of the Vice Chancellor.
o The Dean FoET will issue necessary directions for implementation of the ordinances and other decisions.
o The Dean FoET will monitor the activities of various chapters including student chapters.
o The vision document of MITS-DU will be prepared by the Dean FoET in consultation with other important stakeholders.
o Review of the feedback/survey formats/questionnaires and update from time to time for internal/external stakeholders will be carried out by the Dean FoET.
o The Dean FoET will maintain the records of Doctoral and Post Doctoral students.
o The Dean FoET will be responsible for the conduct and maintenance of the standards of teaching, research and human resource of the MITS-DU and shall have such other functions as may be prescribed by the rules of the lnstitution Deemed to be University and assigned by Vice Chancellor/Pro Chancellor & Chancellor.

Dean (Quality Assurance):
o Will coordinate the MITS-DU's quality assurance system including strategic planning, assessment and evaluation for continuous improvement, statistical analysis of instrumentation to establish validity and reliability and data dissemination.
o Will act as Management Representative for ISO activities and will be responsible for restructuring / revising of the Quality Management System with a focus on NBA, NAAC, NIRF and other such requirements.
o Will look after the NBA, NAAC, Autonomy and ISO reports and other such documentation and preparations.
o Will ensure overall planning and implementation of quality assurance and quality enhancement activities within the MITS-DU.
o Will work for promotion, enhancement and dissemination of good teaching and learning practices and will monitor the regular class room teaching in Face to Face (FF)/blended mode.
o Will spearhead the activities of lnternal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) towards assertion of quality in consultation with Dean FoET.
o Supervise & maintain quality of food served in canteens & Hostel Mess.
o Other work assigned by Dean FoET/V|ce Chancellor.

Dean (Academics):
o To ensure adherence to the ordinances as approved by the Academic Council.
o To Conduct meetings and monitor academic activities in consultation with Dean FoET.
o To conduct the meetings of academic cells.
o To prepare the MITS-DU Academic Calendar.
o To advise examination body regarding the academic rules and practices.
o Plan expansion of academic programmes.
o To work for lnternationalization / National Collaborations in the field of academics.
o To supervise the registration of students through a duly constituted committee.
o To supervise the awards of Medals for Meritorious students.
o To create more Centers of Excellence (CoE) and monitor collaborative activities.
o To monitor the upkeep and upgradation of library and other learning resources.
o To chair the meetings pertaining to academics on behalf of the Dean FoET.
o To address other matters as may be referred by the Dean FoET.
o He/She will report to Dean Faculty of Engineering & Technology.
o Any other work assigned by Dean FoET/Vice Chancellor.

Dean (Research):
o To advise and device policies for various Faculty and Staff Development programmes for qualification upgradation in consultation with Dean FoET.
o To enhance lndustry MITS-DU linkages, collaborative research, entrepreneurship and incubation centers.
o To motivate faculty to write and apply for sponsored research projects.
o To monitor the effective utilization of funds of externally funding projects.
o To maintain and update records of research publications.
o To ensure sponsoring faculty members for attending Conferences/Seminars/ Workshops as per MITS-DU norms.
o To chair the meetings pertaining to research on behalf of the Dean FoET.
o He/She will report to Dean Faculty of Engineering & Technology.
o Any other work assigned by Dean FoET/Vlce Chaneelior.

Dean (Public Relations):
o To liaison between parents, outside world and MITS-DU administration.
o To look after the dissemination of information to outside world including print media, social media and other agencies.
o To supervise the admission process of students at lnstitutional level through duly constituted committees.
o To Coordinate Community Development programmes and social development activities.
o Act as University-lndustry interface including publicity, news bulletins, brochures preparation/distribution, etc.
o To obtain and analyse feedback from students, other internal stakeholders, industries / parents and conduct exit survey for outgoing batch and alumni tracer studies for passed out batches.
o He/She will report to Dean Faculty of Engineering & Technology/Vice Chancellor.
o Other work assigned by the Dean FoET/Vice Chancellor.

Dean (Student Administration):
o To head the discipline committee of the MITS-DU.
o To maintain campus discipline of the students and coordinate the same with the Chief Warden in the smooth functioning of the hostels.
o To work as Chairperson of the Proctorial Committee to prevent ragging.
o To encourage healthy living and lively positive relationships among students and the campus community.
o To analyse placement data to gather an understanding for future trends and articulate needs for university level planning.
o Regularly meet with faculty members to solicit the expertise in placement activities with regard to initializing and planning for future activities.
o To interact with students to keep them informed about the latest market trends and skills & demands so that they can update themselves and prepare best for their career.
o To boost up student morale and counsel them before they undergo summer and final placement activities.
o To provide feedback to faculty members on placement opportunities.
o To build relationships with alumni through seminars, annual meets, etc.
o To plan for accommodation of parents and family members of students during Admission, Convocation, etc.
o To interact with the medical and psychological staff.
o To draft policies, rules & regulations for the functioning of the students.
o To take proper steps in respect of the grievances of students regarding the facilities available to them in the libraries, hostel common rooms and health centres.
o He/She will report to Vice Chancellor.
o Any other work assigned by the Vice Chancellor.

Dean (Student Welfare):
o To promote extra-curricular activities such as games, sports, N.C.C. etc.
o To coordinate between students and administration for extracurricular and co-curricular and Plan and organize various co- and extra-curricular activities.
o To work on the annual budget allocated for the Students Affairs and activities through Clubs/ Societies including Sports and Tech fests and manage spending in a manner that develops policies regarding student Iife.
o Manage, and act as Faculty Advisor for Students events.
o To liaison between university administrators and student organizations, such as student clubs and societies etc. on campus.
o Monitor day-to-day essential support required for co-curricular activities of students.
o Arrange for special care for the weaker and needy sections of students.
o Prepare plan and execute programmes for holistic development of the students.
o Nominate student representatives to various bodies of the University in consultation with Dean, Student Administration.
o Co-ordinate with the office of the Chief Warden in the smooth functioning of the hostels.
o Enable and facilitate conduct of sports and cultural events on the campus.
o Co-ordinate with the sports and cultural in-charges and committees etc. for all matters to students' welfare.
o Students to participate events/programmes outside the University in consultation with Dean, Student Administration.
o Keep in touch with the guardians as and when required.
o Co-ordinate with the Dean, Student Administration on matters related to students' discipline in the University.
o To promote and encourage cultural, social and literary activities with a view to fostering health, corporate life in the student community.
o To establish and direct programmes for guidance and counselling of students.
o He/She will report to Dean Student Administration/Vice Chancellor.
o Any other work assigned by Dean, Student Administration/Vice Chancellor.

Registrar:
o Will be ln-charge of Student Section of the MITS-DU.
o To monitor the admissions of all courses in the MITS-DU.
o To create a mechanism for maintaining all records / files of the students.
o To maintain academic records as per the requirement under rules.
o To prepare class-wise roll lists and distribute them to various departments prior to commencement of the semesters.
o To arrange the scholarships of students under various categories from various sources and accounting of the same.
o To verify / issue the pass-out students, their transcripts, TC, Character Certificates.
o To liaison between University, AICTE, DST, DTE, UGC and other Government agencies for the administrative and financial matters of the MITS-DU.
o To arrange for the Approvals/Affiliation of University and approval of programmes from various Government Agencies.
o To maintain the discipline of the students of the MITS-DU.
o Registrar will be custodian of Students' related records i.e., files, results, merit lists, transcripts, schemes and Syllabus.
o Registrar will issue purchase orders.
o Will sign contracts of various agencies on behalf of Vice Chancellor.
o Will look after the Legal Cases of the MITS-DU related to students & other assigned by Vice Chancellor.
o He/She will report to Vice Chancellor.
o Any other work assigned from time to time by the Vice Chancellor.

Controller (Examination) and Dy. Controller (Examination):
o To ensure timely evaluation process, (continuous as well as end-term), with adherence to guidelines and norms.
o To prepare the time table, monitor activities such as question paper printing, answer sheet distribution and record maintenance etc. to ensure affair and ethical examination.
o To ensure that that the paper setting and paper quality assurance systems are in place.
