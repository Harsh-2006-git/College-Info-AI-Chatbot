Key Guidelines of Internship Policy:
- Mandatory Internship Requirement: Every student is required to complete mandatory industrial training/internships (typically 6-8 weeks during summer/winter breaks or a full semester in final year for selected tracks).
- NOC and Approval: Students must obtain prior approval and a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the T&P Cell and respective Department Head before joining an external internship.
- Evaluation: Internships are evaluated through project reports, company feedback/evaluation forms, and viva-voce examinations.

Key Guidelines of Placement Policy:
- Registration: All final year students aspiring for campus placement must register with the T&P Cell and upload verified academic credentials.
- Dream / Core / Mass Recruiter Categorization: Institute defines clear guidelines for offer acceptance, Dream company opportunities, and multi-offer rules.
- Discipline & Code of Conduct: Registered students must strictly adhere to professional interview attire, punctuality, and attendance for drives they have opted in for. Any violation or unexcused absence after shortlisting invites disciplinary action.
