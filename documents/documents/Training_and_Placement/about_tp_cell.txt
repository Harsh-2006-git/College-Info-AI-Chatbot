OVERVIEW & MISSION:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Goal of the Training & Placement Cell of MITS Gwalior is to act as a vital liaison between industry and students to provide employment opportunities available in the market and to provide intensive training to students for their overall career development.
It works under the guidance of the Training & Placement Officer in consultation with the Faculty In-Charge and student placement coordinators.

DISCIPLINES COVERED:
Training & Placement Cell provides comprehensive placement support to students across all branches and programs:
- Civil Engineering
- Mechanical Engineering
- Electrical Engineering
- Electronics & Communication Engineering
- Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering
- Computer Science & Engineering
- Information Technology
- Artificial Intelligence & Data Science / Machine Learning
- Internet of Things (IoT) & Mathematics and Computing
- Chemical Engineering & Biotechnology
- Architecture & Planning (B.Arch & MUP)
- Computer Applications (MCA) & Business Administration (MBA)

RECRUITMENT LOGISTICS & SERVICES:
Right from connecting with leading companies in the industry to managing all logistics of arranging the whole campus recruitment process with professional hospitality; T&P Cell officials and student coordinators provide seamless assistance to recruiters.
Apart from campus placements, the cell focuses on:
- Soft & professional skills training for Pre-Final & Final year students.
- Mandatory industrial projects, internships & practical field training.
- Guest lectures, motivational sessions, and technical seminars.
- Alumni connect programmes to build and enhance the brand value of MITS Gwalior.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TRAINING & PLACEMENT CELL ACTIVITIES:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Campus Placements Process:
   Campus recruitment drives are organized annually by inviting top multinational corporations, Fortune 500 firms, Indian conglomerates, and tech startups. T&P Cell arranges world-class infrastructure including auditorium for pre-placement talks, computer labs for online assessments, group discussion chambers, and interview rooms. Companies can send job descriptions and recruitment details directly to: tnp@mitsgwalior.in.

2. Career Orientation Programmes:
   - Intensive training modules in soft skills, aptitude test preparation, coding platforms, technical interview preparation, group discussions, and resume writing.
   - Expert sessions and masterclasses by senior industry leaders orienting students to current industry expectations and future tech trends.

3. Industrial Training, Internships & Visits:
   - Mandatory 6 to 8-week industrial training and internships to expose students to real-world engineering challenges.
   - Organized industrial visits with faculty members to manufacturing plants, IT campuses, construction sites, and research centers.
   - Industry-defined capstone projects reflecting contemporary technological problems.

WHY RECRUIT FROM MITS GWALIOR?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Legacy of Excellence: Established in 1957, MITS is one of the oldest and most respected premier engineering institutions in the country, renowned alongside premier institutes of national importance.
- NAAC A++ Accredited Deemed University: Recognized for exceptional academic rigour, world-class laboratory infrastructure, and research culture.
- Industry-Aligned Curriculum: Flexible credit system with specialized tracks in AI/ML, Data Science, IoT, Cloud Computing, Cybersecurity, Robotics, and Green Energy.
- Vibrant Holistic Ecosystem: Rich extra-curricular culture including technical festivals, hackathons, national coding competitions, cultural fests, and state-of-the-art sports facilities.
