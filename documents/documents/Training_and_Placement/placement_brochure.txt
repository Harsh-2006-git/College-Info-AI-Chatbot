Training & Placements — Placement Brochure
Madhav Institute of Technology & Science (MITS), Gwalior

OVERVIEW:
The MITS Gwalior Placement Brochure provides prospective recruiters with a complete overview of the institute's academic curriculum, department-wise student demographics, laboratory infrastructure, R&D achievements, past recruitment trends, and step-by-step procedures for conducting campus recruitment drives.

1. Message from the Vice Chancellor & Training & Placement Officer.
2. Academic pedagogy, autonomous curriculum, and grading system.
3. Branch-wise student intake and specialization matrices (UG, PG, Ph.D.).
4. Departmental laboratory equipment and industry-sponsored Centers of Excellence.
5. Campus placement process, interview facilities, accommodation, and hospitality for visiting corporate delegates.
6. Contact details of T&P officials and faculty placement advisors.
