Laboratories

Vibration Analysis Lab
The Reliability Training Centre consists of the latest equipment in bearing maintenance and condition monitoring fields which shall give a unique opportunity to students to attend hands-on training programs and practical sharing of knowledge.
Major Equipment: Mounting star (Bearing training kit), Bearing Mounting and Dismounting kit, Alignment rig with tools, Basic condition monitoring kit, Self-learning tool, FFT Analyser, Experimental test rigs, Online Learning tools.

I C Engine Lab
Internal Combustion Engine Laboratory is a well-equipped laboratory that provides ideas on the practice of handling IC engines and measuring the performance parameters. This laboratory is scheduled for 4th-semester students of Automobile Engineering and 6th-semester students of the Mechanical engineering branch. The maximum tests which are performed on IC engines are for commercial purposes to find the following: The overload carrying capacity of the engine. The quantity of cooling water required on a brake power basis in kg per kW-hr. Brake Power (which is also known as rated power) with guaranteed fuel consumption.

Refrigeration & Air Conditioning Lab
The Refrigeration and Air-conditioning Lab consists of modern equipment in Vapour Compression Refrigeration System, Air-conditioning training system as well as various refrigeration test rigs which makes a unique platform for the students to perform their experimental practice in the refrigeration area.
Major Equipment: Vapour compression refrigeration trainer, Cold storage trainer, Air conditioning test rig, Duct able air conditioning test rig, cut section of various transmission, vapour compression refrigeration system, Direct and indirect type AC & Refrigeration and fault simulator

Mechanics of Material Lab
Mechanical of Material Lab is currently engaging courses namely Fluid Mechanics & Hydraulic Machines (120314/190314) for Mechanical and Automobile branches respectively. This lab is run in conjunction with the theory course 120319/190317 (Mechanical of Material) under the first Department Core Subject. Mechanics of Materials is one of the important subjects in Mechanical as well as in Automotive Engineering. It is the physical science that looks at the reaction of a body to movement and deformation due to mechanical, thermal, and other loads.
Major Equipment: Universal Testing Machine, Rockwell Hardness Testing Machine, Extensometer, Fatigue Testing Machine, Pendulum Impact Tester, Surface Roughness Tester, Bending Moment Apparatus, Shear force Apparatus)

Heat & Mass Transfer Lab
Heat and Mass Transfer laboratory provides fundamental and industrial knowledge about modes of heat and mass transfer, like conduction, convection, radiation, mass transfer, and their application. The experimental setups in this laboratory are a part of the curriculum for third-year undergraduate students undertaking the course “Heat and Mass Transfer”. To enhance the practical knowledge of industrial equipment, students perform the practical on Shell-&-Tube heat exchanger, Parallel flow heat exchanger, finding Conductivity of metal rod and insulating powder, experiments to determine convective heat transfer coefficient in various materials, the performance of pin fin of various materials apparatus and experiment to understand Kirchhoff’s and Stefan Boltzman laws.

Basic Mechanical Engineering Lab
Mechanical Engineering is often considered the most basic type of engineering work. It is probably the first branch of engineering which has vast applications in all fields. Mechanical engineers were responsible for Industrial Revolution and innovations like the steam engine, internal combustion engines, turbines, compressors, pneumatic machines, machine tools, refrigeration, air conditioning systems, etc done by mechanical engineers. Therefore every student should know the basics of mechanical engineering.
