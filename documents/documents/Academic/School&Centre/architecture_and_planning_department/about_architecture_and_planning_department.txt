Department: Department of Architecture & Planning
Madhav Institute of Technology & Science (MITS), Gwalior

Department Vision:
To create innovative professional ARCHITECTS who are rooted in our cultural ethos contributing wholesomely to nation building.

Department Mission:
- To develop talent through issue based academics and philosophical approach to design.
- To impart state of the art training for contemporary needs of design and construction of buildings.
- To encompass multidisciplinary perspective, nurturing students' critical thinking, creativity and problem-solving skills.
- To provide global leadership in the field of architecture, planning and allied fields.

About the Department:
Architecture is the only course with an amalgamation of Technology, Science and Art. The Department of Architecture was established in the year 1984 with an intake of 20 students and was later enhanced to 40 students in the year 2004. The department has in place a high quality of resources and infrastructure to impart architectural and urban planning education.

Extended Vision:
"To create world class quality Engineers and Technocrats capable of providing leadership in all spheres of life and society."
The Department aspires to lead in the arena and era of future research in architecture and its allied fields. The contribution towards societal needs and national importance will be the highest priority of the department for the future. To cater to the needs of academia, sponsored and non-sponsored extension services are handled with high precision and competence.
