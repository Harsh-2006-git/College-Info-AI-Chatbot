Department Vision
To nurture globally competent, ethical, and innovative Computer Professionals for societal and technological advancement through innovation, research, and sustainability.

Department Mission
- To provide global benchmark education through Outcome-Based Education (OBE), nurturing strong computing, analytical, and problem-solving skills for developing future-ready professionals.
- To provide industry-oriented learning through innovative teaching practices and modern laboratories, enabling learners to develop global competencies in emerging technologies.
- To foster research, innovation, and entrepreneurship leading to Intellectual Property Rights (IPR), quality publications, interdisciplinary collaborations, and technological advancements.
- To develop innovative and ethically responsible engineers by providing a dynamic learning environment that emphasizes sustainability, lifelong learning, leadership, and excellence in emerging technological domains.
