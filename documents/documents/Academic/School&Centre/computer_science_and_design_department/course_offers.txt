Programmes Offered — Department of Computer Science and Design (CSD)
=====================================================================

Undergraduate Programme
-----------------------
Course Name: B.Tech Computer Science & Design (CSD)
Duration: 4-Year Degree Course (8 Semesters)
Intake: 120 Seats
Type: Regular Full-time Degree Programme
Affiliation / Institution: Madhav Institute of Technology & Science (MITS - Deemed University), Gwalior
Overview: The department offers a comprehensive B.Tech curriculum covering topics related to computer hardware and software design with an emphasis on practical learning, modern frameworks, data science, machine learning, and emerging design technologies.
