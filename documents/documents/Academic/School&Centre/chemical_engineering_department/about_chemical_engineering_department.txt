Department: Chemical Engineering Department
Madhav Institute of Technology & Science (MITS), Gwalior

Department Vision:
To be a leader in Chemical Engineering Education and Research by providing balanced learning and fostering research to enable the learners to meet the challenges of process industries and societal needs.

Department Mission:
- Share state of the art knowledge and facilities and enable optimal utilization of the resources in the region.
- To adopt good pedagogical practices and ethics in order to achieve excellence.
- Develop research culture and create a platform to disseminate research and development.

About the Department:
The Chemical Engineering Department was started in 1996 and offers B.Tech/B.E. and M.Tech courses in Chemical Engineering. The department was started with 30 students in undergraduate studies, but later, recognizing the importance and growing industry demand for Chemical Engineers, the intake capacity was raised to 60 in 2013-14. The M.Tech course was started in 2013-14 with specialization in Chemical Engineering. The department was founded with the vision of developing into an academic center of excellence in Chemical Engineering and associated areas, preparing competitive professionals and experts equipped with knowledge, skills, and ethical attitude to serve the society and nation.
