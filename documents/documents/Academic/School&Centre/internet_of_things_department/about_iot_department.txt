About the Centre for Internet of Things
The Centre for Internet of Things (CIoT) was established in 2023 with the vision of becoming a hub for innovation, research, and skill development in the rapidly evolving domain of Internet of Things.
The centre is offering two B.Tech. courses: "B.Tech in Internet of Things (IoT) and B.Tech in Electrical and Computer Engineering."
The centre aims to bridge the gap between academia and industry by providing hands-on training, interdisciplinary learning, and real-world problem-solving experiences.
The centre is equipped with advanced infrastructure, IoT development kits, sensors, microcontrollers, and cloud-enabled platforms to support innovative student projects, faculty research, industry collaborations, and real-world applications in smart agriculture, healthcare, smart cities, and Industry 4.0 technologies.
The centre serves as a platform to nurture entrepreneurial talent and develop IoT-enabled solutions that contribute to societal and industrial advancement.

Department Vision
To emerge as a center of excellence in the field of Internet of Things by driving innovation, interdisciplinary research, and skill development to empower technocrats who can lead digital transformation and deliver sustainable solutions for society.

Department Mission
- To provide quality education and practical exposure in IoT technologies through interdisciplinary and experiential learning.
- To cultivate innovation and entrepreneurship by developing IoT-based solutions for real-world challenges across various domains.
- To establish collaborations with industry, academia, and research institutions for advanced IoT research, training, and consultancy.
- To promote impactful research, publications, and skill-building programs that contribute to the global IoT ecosystem.
