Department: School of Humanities and Management
Madhav Institute of Technology & Science (MITS), Gwalior

Department Vision:
To impart knowledge of subjects in Humanities that serves to complete the academic potential of a graduate student in the engineering stream.

Department Mission:
- To replace Communication Skills with Soft Skills - a paper with a more practical approach in improving communication and behavioural skills of students.
- To initiate papers in psychological and behavioural science so as to provide students the needed assistance in realising their goals.
- To initiate certificate and degree programmes so as to convert itself into a full-fledged department.
- To emphasise Entrepreneurship to enable students to generate employment instead of merely becoming employees.

About the Department:
The stream of Humanities has been an integral part of the Engineering Curricula since its inception, and the Department has delivered these courses with variations in syllabi and schemes according to the evolving needs of the times and student requirements. The Department at present has five faculty members and organizes additional and remedial classes for students in need.

Additional Classes Handled:
1. Special classes in Communication Skills and Personality Development.
2. English as a foundation course open to all students.

Extended Vision & Mission:
The Department engages classes for courses taught at both Undergraduate and Postgraduate levels. As it is imperative for every student to display proficiency in Language and Managerial Skills to be an effective executive, the aim of the Department is to inculcate the habit of speaking fluently, presenting views effectively, and developing strong leadership qualities. The Department seeks to achieve the all-round development of students by grooming their personality in both hard and soft skills. This enables students and faculty alike to pursue higher studies and advanced research for societal betterment and national advancement.
