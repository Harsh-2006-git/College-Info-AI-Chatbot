Programme Educational Objectives (PEOs)
PEO I The graduate of the program will have successful career in various domains of civil engineering.
PEO II The graduate of the program will be able to practice civil engineering in infrastructure industries, public sector undertakings and as an entrepreneur.
PEO III The graduate of the program will be able to pursue higher education for knowledge enhancement.
PEO IV The graduate of the program will be able to exhibit leadership skills, team spirit and ethical practices.

Program Outcomes (POs)
PO I Engineering Knowledge: The graduate will be able to apply knowledge of mathematics, science & design principles to solve the problems in civil engineering.
PO II Problem Analysis: The graduate will be able to identify, formulate and analyse engineering problems with substantial conclusions using analytical tools appropriate to civil engineering.
PO III Design/Development of Solutions: The graduate will be able to design civil engineering systems within realistic constraints to meet desired needs of economy, environment, society, health and safety.
PO IV Conduct Investigations of Complex Problems: The graduate will be able to conduct investigations of complex problems by use of research based knowledge & methods including experimentation, interpretation of data & synthesis of information to provide valid conclusion.
PO V Modern Tool Usage: The graduate will be able to use the technical skills and modern engineering / computational tools for broadly defining engineering activities.
PO VI The Engineer and Society: The graduate will be able to assess safety & legal issues and the consequent responsibilities relevant to the professional civil engineering practice.
PO VII Environment and Sustainability: The graduate will be able to understand the impact of the professional civil engineering solutions in relation to societal needs, environmental concern & sustainable development.
PO VIII Ethics: The graduate will be able to understand the importance of professional ethics and norms of civil engineering practice.
PO IX Individual and Team work: The graduate will be able to function effectively as a member or leader in diverse teams
PO X Communication: The graduate will be able to communicate effectively with engineering community and society at large by being able to comprehend and write effective reports, prepare documentation and make effective presentation.
PO XI Project management and Finance: The graduate will be able to demonstrate knowledge of management and financial principles to civil engineering projects.
PO XII Life-long Learning: The graduate will be able to engage in lifelong learning & adapt to rapid changes in civil engineering and its allied areas.

Program Specific Outcomes (PSO)
PSO I The graduate will be able practice codal provisions like IRC, IS, NBC, CPHEEO manuals for planning & designing of civil infrastructures.
PSO II The graduate will be able to understand the importance of uncertainty & risk involved in infrastructure projects and subsequently propose the solutions.
