Honours and Minors

Honour Specialization
A degree with “Honour” simply signifies academic excellence, indicating extra efforts in acquiring 20 additional credits through MOOC based platform (from the pool of courses recommended by the Board of Studies) within the permitted duration of the degree.

Minor Specialization
The student can opt for “Minor Specialization” in other disciplines by acquiring 20 additional credits through MOOC based platform (from the pool of courses recommended by the Board of Studies) within the permitted duration of the degree.
