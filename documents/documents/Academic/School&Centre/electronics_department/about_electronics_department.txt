Department of Electronics Engineering — MITS Gwalior
======================================================

Department Vision
-----------------
To Prepare Globally Competent Electronics Engineers for Industrial Challenges and Societal needs.

Department Mission
------------------
- To provide quality education through need based curriculum and effective teaching learning process.
- To imbibe professional ethics, leadership and entrepreneur skills with a passion for lifelong learning.
- Strengthening of employability skills through multidisciplinary projects and internships.
- To inculcate the spirit of innovation and research to serve the needs of the society and industry.

Accreditation
-------------
Electronics Engineering program is NBA-accredited till 2028.

About the Department
--------------------
The Department of Electronics Engineering was established in 1987. The department offers a UG programme in Electronics Engineering with an intake of 120 students, a PG programme in M.Tech. Embedded System and VLSI with an intake of 18 students, and a Ph.D. programme. 

The faculty members are actively engaged in cutting-edge research in the areas of Ad-hoc Networking, Image Processing, Wireless Communication, Microwave Engineering, Nano-electronics, VLSI, and Antenna Theory. Faculty members have published numerous papers in reputed National and International Journals.

The alumni of the department have secured prestigious positions in the higher echelons of society and the global technical world. A substantial number of graduates pursue higher studies for their M.Tech, MBA, and Ph.D. from premier institutes in India and abroad.
