Department: School of Applied Mathematics
Madhav Institute of Technology & Science (MITS), Gwalior

Department Vision:
To be a school of excellence in Applied Mathematics by imparting high-quality mathematical education, fostering analytical and critical thinking, promoting interdisciplinary research, and developing innovative solutions to engineering, scientific, and societal challenges.

Department Mission:
- Impart quality education in applied mathematics with emphasis on engineering applications and computational techniques.
- Foster analytical and critical thinking, innovation, and interdisciplinary research to solve complex engineering problems.
- Develop mathematical modelling, analytical reasoning, and computational skills through modern tools and experiential learning.
- Encourage professional ethics, lifelong learning, entrepreneurship, and collaboration with industry and research organizations.

About the Department:
The School of Applied Mathematics is dedicated to providing a strong foundation in mathematical sciences and their practical applications across engineering, technology, science, and interdisciplinary domains. The School offers high-quality education in core areas such as calculus, linear algebra, differential equations, probability, statistics, numerical methods, optimization, and computational mathematics. With a focus on analytical thinking, mathematical modeling, and problem-solving, the School prepares students to address complex real-world challenges. 

Faculty members actively engage in teaching, research, and consultancy in emerging areas, fostering innovation and interdisciplinary collaboration. Through a blend of academic excellence and research-driven learning, the School of Applied Mathematics plays a vital role in supporting the academic and research activities of the Institute while equipping students with the quantitative skills required for higher studies, research, and professional careers.
