Department: Centre for Artificial Intelligence (Artificial Intelligence Department)
Madhav Institute of Technology & Science (MITS), Gwalior

Department Vision
To become a distinguished centre in Artificial Intelligence, empowering innovation, research and creating solutions to address real-world challenges with societal relevance.

Department Mission
- Deliver a cutting-edge curriculum in AI, AIML, AIDS, and AIR, empowering students with the skills to solve real-world challenges and lead in global technology.
- Promote a culture of research and innovation in AI, enabling impactful collaborations with industry and academia for technological advancement.
- Equip students to apply AI responsibly, ensuring ethical, societal, and human-centered solutions are at the core of their innovations.

About Artificial Intelligence Department / Centre for Artificial Intelligence
The Centre for Artificial Intelligence was Established in the year 2023. The objective is to nurture academic excellence, industry exposure and outstanding career opportunities to our students. The department consists of highly qualified faculty with doctoral degrees from reputed institutes of India and abroad. The department aims to train students as leaders in AI, DS & Robotics with world-class infrastructure, curriculum, and comprehensive hands-on experience through industry connect programs. The goal of the centre is to ensure academic excellence in students with wide exposure of research and career opportunities.
