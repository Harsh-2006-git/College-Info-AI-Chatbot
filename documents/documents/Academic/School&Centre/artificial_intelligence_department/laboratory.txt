Laboratories

1. Robotics Lab
The Robotics Laboratory is a state-of-the-art facility established to promote innovation, research, and hands-on learning in robotics, artificial intelligence, autonomous systems, and intelligent machines. The laboratory is equipped with advanced infrastructure, including 3D Printers, Robotic Arms, High-Performance Computing (HPC) Systems, NVIDIA Jetson Nano Development Boards, and Aurion Robotics Kits, enabling students and researchers to design, prototype, and deploy robotic solutions.
The laboratory provides comprehensive facilities for the design, development, testing, and deployment of both humanoid and non-humanoid robots, catering to applications in automation, healthcare, manufacturing, agriculture, and smart environments. It also supports the development of Drones and Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) for aerial surveillance, mapping, monitoring, and autonomous navigation applications.
Students utilize the laboratory for academic projects, research activities, product prototyping, and participation in national and international competitions. The lab has a strong track record of excellence, with student teams winning numerous hackathons, robotics competitions, and innovation challenges, demonstrating their technical competence and innovative capabilities.
The Robotics Laboratory serves as a multidisciplinary innovation hub, fostering collaboration among students, faculty members, industry experts, and startups while encouraging the development of next-generation intelligent robotic systems and autonomous technologies.

2. Programming Lab 1
Programming Lab 1 provides a computing platform for novel languages like Python and R for building a coherent set of ideas, fundamentals of Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, and Deep Learning models and algorithms, and it facilitates students to practice basic programming in C, C++, and JAVA also. Each lab session is aimed to translate the theory lectures into practical implementation through programming paradigms and tools, and platforms provided in the data science lab.

3. Programming Lab 2
Programming Lab 2 welcomes students, researchers, interns, faculties, newbies, and everyone from a broad range of disciplines who are fascinated with the possibilities of turning big and complex data into knowledge. Members of the data science lab seek cutting-edge problem-based solutions to data mining, computer vision, and machine learning problems arising in numerous application areas involving data analytics.

4. High Performance Computing (HPC) Lab
HPC Lab is equipped with a wide range of powerful computers with high-end graphics cards that are essential for research. The HPC Lab brings together researchers interested in developing fast, reliable, and robust numerical algorithms needed for modeling and simulation in a variety of fields including database management, Numerical linear algebra, Optimization, etc. The requirement of modern days is to have an automated system that manages, modifies, and updates data accurately. The lab aims at practicing and achieving this aim by using various software. All these require “hands-on” training in programming through practical projects.

5. Augmented Reality & Virtual Reality Lab
The Augmented Reality (AR) and Virtual Reality (VR) Laboratory is an advanced interdisciplinary research and innovation facility dedicated to immersive technologies, human-computer interaction, and intelligent systems. The laboratory is equipped with cutting-edge infrastructure, including Meta Quest Headsets, Microsoft HoloLens Devices, Brain Signal Mapping Systems, High-Performance Computing (HPC) Systems, and Unity Development Software, enabling students and researchers to develop next-generation AR, VR, Mixed Reality (MR), and Extended Reality (XR) applications.
The laboratory provides a comprehensive environment for the design, development, and deployment of immersive digital experiences across diverse domains such as education, healthcare, industrial training, smart manufacturing, entertainment, and simulation. Students actively engage in AR/VR application development, 3D environment design, digital twin creation, immersive visualization, and interactive user experience development using industry-standard tools and platforms.
A unique research focus of the laboratory is the integration of Brain-Computer Interface (BCI) technologies, where students and researchers work on projects involving brain signal acquisition, mapping, and interpretation for controlling robotic systems and intelligent devices through neural signals. This facilitates research in assistive technologies, neuro-robotics, and human-machine interaction.
The laboratory has contributed significantly to research and innovation, resulting in the publication of numerous research papers in reputed national and international journals and conferences. Student teams associated with the laboratory have consistently demonstrated excellence by securing top positions in various national-level hackathons, innovation challenges, and technology competitions across India.
The AR/VR Laboratory serves as a center of excellence for immersive technologies, fostering innovation, interdisciplinary collaboration, research-driven learning, and the development of future-ready solutions at the intersection of virtual reality, artificial intelligence, robotics, and neuroscience.
